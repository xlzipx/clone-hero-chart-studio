Lyrics — controls & format

Writing & splitting words

One line = one phrase. A space starts a new syllable. To split a word across beats, end a syllable with - (glue, no gap) or = (keep a visible hyphen).
ev- ery sing- le day → “every single day” (4 syllables you can time separately)
well= known → “well-known”
Tip: double-click a syllable on the karaoke line to edit it or split it into more — type spaces / - right there.

Snap — where a syllable lands

You never type a tick. The position comes from the playhead (Enter) or from dragging a dot, and the Snap button in the right column decides how it settles:
free → exactly where you dropped it
1/8, 1/16 … → onto the grid — the same division the note tools use
note → onto the nearest note in this track (within one beat) — vocals sit on the rhythm, so this usually nails it
Then walk the song with ↑ ↓ and fix whatever drifted with ← →. The header shows the exact time of the syllable you are standing on.

What the colours mean

Next up — Enter places this one Playing now — under the playhead, same orange the game uses Sung — already gone by in this phrase Placed — timed, still ahead Behind you — earlier phrases on the timeline Queued — no timing yet

Difficulties

Copy a difficulty of the current instrument, or auto-reduce it into an easier one (thinner grid, simpler chords).
→
Hard = 1/8 grid, max 2-note chords · Medium = 1/4 grid, single notes · Easy = 1/2 grid, single notes. Drums also drop ghosts/accents below Hard. Star Power and solos carry over.
